# Coupling with WLED AudioReactive

`wled-usermod-audio-classifier` is designed from the ground up to **coexist symbiotically** with WLED's native `AudioReactive` usermod rather than competing with it for hardware resources.

---

## 💡 How Coexistence Works

1. **Zero Hardware Conflicts**:
   Commercial WLED controllers (such as the Gledopto GL-C-010WL / GL-C-015WL-D) or custom ESP32 + I2S microphone boards have a single physical microphone. Only one peripheral can act as I2S master clock driver. `AudioReactive` acts as the master, acquiring audio and computing 16-channel Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT).

2. **Non-Invasive In-Memory Telemetry**:
   Through WLED's internal `UsermodManager::getUMData()` API, `AudioClassifier` reads the real-time smoothed volume, raw unscaled volume, 16 frequency channels, beat/peak flags, and dominant peak frequencies generated by AudioReactive.

3. **LED Effects Preserved**:
   Your audio-reactive LED strip effects continue running at full frame rate with zero visual stutter or degradation.

---

## ⚙️ Recommended AudioReactive Settings

For optimal acoustic presence and state classification, open WLED **Config > Usermods > AudioReactive** and configure:

| Setting | Recommended Value | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`enabled`** | `Checked` | Required for audio acquisition. |
| **`AGC` (Automatic Gain Control)** | `0 (Off)` or `1 (Normal)` | When AGC is off, relative level (%) represents true room sound dynamics. If AGC is vivid or lazy, dynamic range will float. |
| **`squelch`** | `5` – `15` | Baseline noise gate setting. |
| **`gain`** | `40` – `80` | Adjust until normal speaking volume sits around 15–35% on the AudioClassifier status line. |

## 📡 Diagnostic Statuses

The `sensor.[id]_audio_status` entity in Home Assistant and the WLED Web UI info page will report one of the following diagnostic states:

- **`OK (Coupled with AudioReactive)`**: Everything is running normally.
- **`Warning: AudioReactive is disabled in settings`**: Check the box next to `AudioReactive` in Usermods settings.
- **`Warning: AudioReactive AGC is enabled (relative levels will float)`**: Relative level (%) will adjust automatically with AGC multiplier.
- **`Info: AudioReactive is in network receive mode`**: Audio data is being streamed from another WLED instance across WiFi.
- **`Warning: No audio signal detected from AudioReactive (>30s)`**: Check your physical microphone wiring or solder joints.
- **`Error: AudioReactive usermod not present in build`**: Ensure firmware was compiled with `AudioReactive` included.
